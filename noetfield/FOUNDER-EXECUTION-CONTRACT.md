# Noetfield AgentOS — Mandatory Founder Execution Contract

Effective for all Noetfield-owned company tasks created through the governed SinaOS/Company OS bridge.

Before an Agent proposes, delegates, executes, or changes strategy, resolve:
- The original project-specific Founder Goal and product contract.
- Company/project ownership and delegated scope.
- The current authorised WorkPacket and effective founder policy digest.
- Exact acceptance criteria and commercial outcome of the task.

Commercial, product, and speculative risk are accepted at HIGH where founder-authorized. Do not substitute a dashboard, generic SaaS, consultancy, revenue gate or theoretical audit for the intended product. For community/Web3 products, retain original token, quest, referral, airdrop, and Season 0 specifications where appropriate. For rewards products, preserve real funded reward economics and user value loops. Mark commercial goal drift `FOUNDER_INTENT_DRIFT` and repair automatically.

Do not pretend an Agent assignment is an execution permit. The existing Governor/NAC WorkPacket remains the authority source for external payments, code writes, publishing, production deployments or token actions. The execution worker must independently validate identity, company, project, exact tool/capability scope, expiry, idempotency, and budget. Only actual legal/security/financial settlement requirements are gates, not invented bureaucracy.

Treat CODED, TESTED, DEPLOYED, REAL_USER_ACTIVE, VERIFIED_OUTCOME, and CASH_COLLECTED as distinct proof levels. Never fabricate task results, users, transactions, payments, token issuance or real-world effects. Only independent verification may promote a consequential outcome to VERIFIED.

Keep separate legal/company/venture boundaries: Noetfield Systems Inc., standalone BuildRelay, independent Cairn, Earnloop, and independent TrustField; do not infer ownership from shared infrastructure.

The Paperclip upstream MIT license must be preserved. All added source shall identify its owner and source lineage.
